# ollie-site

The public web pages for [Ollie](https://github.com/SiRiiL-AI/Project_Ollie) — a landing
page, the privacy policy, and the terms of service. Served by GitHub Pages.

**This repository is public on purpose, and deliberately contains no product code.**
Google will not let an OAuth app be published without a publicly reachable privacy policy
and terms of service, and GitHub Pages only serves public repositories on a free plan.
Keeping those three pages here means Ollie's source can stay private while still meeting
Google's requirement — at no cost.

## Live

- <https://siriil-ai.github.io/ollie-site/>
- <https://siriil-ai.github.io/ollie-site/privacy>
- <https://siriil-ai.github.io/ollie-site/terms>

These two URLs are registered on Ollie's Google OAuth consent screen. **If they ever
404, Google can suspend the app** and every user loses their Gmail and Drive connection.
Don't rename the files.

## Editing

Plain Markdown, rendered by GitHub Pages (Jekyll). Push to `main` and it's live in about
a minute.
